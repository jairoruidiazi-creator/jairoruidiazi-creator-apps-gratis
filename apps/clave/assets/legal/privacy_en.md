# Clave privacy policy

Effective October 18, 2026.

## In short

Clave does not collect anyone's data. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking tools, and it does not connect to the internet. Microphone sound is analyzed on the phone, and your recordings, presets and practice log are stored on your device.

## Who publishes the app

Clave is a free, open-source app published by the apps-gratis team under the developer name shown on its Google Play and App Store listings. The source code is at github.com/jairoruidiazi-creator/jairoruidiazi-creator-apps-gratis.

## What information the app handles and where it stays

- **Microphone sound.** The tuner listens to the microphone to work out the note you play. That sound is processed in real time, on the phone, and is neither stored nor sent.
- **Recordings.** When you record a take, the audio is saved as a file in the app's private storage, with the name, folder, notes, instrument and tempo you give it.
- **Metronome presets and setlists.**
- **Practice log:** when and for how long you used each tool, to show your minutes, goals and streak.
- **App settings** (language, theme, tuning, sounds, goals).

All of this lives on your device. We do not receive it, see it or have any way to recover it.

## Permissions

- **Microphone:** only for the tuner and the recorder. If you don't grant it, the rest of the app works the same.
- **Vibration:** to mark the metronome beat, if you turn it on.
- **Background audio (iOS)** and keeping the screen on: so the metronome and drone keep playing while you practice.

## What the app does not do

- It does not use the internet: nothing is sent to our servers or anyone else's.
- It shows no advertising and has no in-app purchases.
- It includes no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

The metronome's spoken count-in uses the device's built-in system voice, which generates the audio on the phone itself.

## Sharing, exporting and importing

You decide when to share a recording or export your presets: the app uses the system share sheet and you choose which app or person receives them. You can also import presets from a file you pick. Whatever you share is subject to the terms of whoever receives it. The app deletes its own copy of exported presets the next time you open it.

## Children

Clave is a music practice tool for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13 and collects no one's data.

## Security

Data stays in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot access. We recommend protecting the device with a screen lock. If system backups are turned on (Google on Android, iCloud on iOS), the system may include the app's data in them; those backups stay in your Google or Apple account, are governed by their terms, and we have no access to them.

## Retention and deletion

Your data stays on the device until you delete it. You can delete recordings and presets in the app, or delete everything by uninstalling it or clearing its data in the system settings. Since we keep no copies, there is nothing else to delete on our side.

## Your rights

Because we do not collect or keep personal data, we hold no information about you to access, correct or delete; you control it directly on the device. This also covers the rights under Colombia's Law 1581 of 2012 and similar laws elsewhere.

## Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles information, we will update this policy and its effective date before publishing the new version. Previous versions remain in the repository history.
