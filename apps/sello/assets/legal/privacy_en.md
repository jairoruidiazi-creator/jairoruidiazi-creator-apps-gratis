# Sello privacy policy

Effective October 18, 2026.

## In short

Sello has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking tools, and we have no servers. Your photos, coordinates and projects are stored on your phone. The only things that leave it, and only if you use those features, are what is needed to draw the map background and to look up a photo's approximate address. Both features can be turned off.

## Who publishes the app

Sello is a free, open-source app published by the apps-gratis team under the developer name shown on its Google Play and App Store listings. The source code is at github.com/jairoruidiazi-creator/jairoruidiazi-creator-apps-gratis.

## What information the app handles and where it stays

- **Photos.** The photos you take are saved in the app's private storage: the stamped copy and, if you leave it on, the original without the stamp.
- **Data for each photo:** date and time, coordinates, altitude, accuracy, heading, approximate address, project, notes, tags and the fields you define. They are written into the stamp on the image and into its metadata (EXIF).
- **Projects, stamp templates, logos you choose and app settings.**
- **Copy in the photo library.** If you leave this option on, Sello also saves a copy of each stamped photo in the phone's photo library (the "Sello" album on Android; the Photos app on iPhone), where apps you give photo access to can see it.

We do not receive any of this, see it or have any way to recover it.

## Permissions

- **Camera:** to take the photos.
- **Location while using the app:** for coordinates, altitude and address. Sello does not use location in the background.
- **Motion sensors:** to work out the camera heading and the level. That data is used on the phone.
- **Photos and storage:** to save the copy in the photo library, if you turn it on. On iPhone, Sello only asks to add photos, not to view them; on Android 10 or earlier the storage permission is used, and from Android 11 no permission is needed. Sello does not read, analyze or upload your other photos.

## What leaves the phone and to whom

- **Approximate address (optional, on by default).** To turn coordinates into an address, Sello uses the operating system's address service, which sends the coordinates to Google (Android) or Apple (iOS) and returns the address. It is governed by Google's and Apple's privacy policies. Turn it off in Settings › Approximate address; coordinates keep working offline.
- **Online map (optional, on by default).** To draw the map background, Sello downloads map images from the OpenStreetMap Foundation's servers, which receive the area you are viewing and your IP address, like any website. It is governed by the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy (osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy). Turn it off in Settings › Online map; the map keeps showing your photos and position.
- **Open in maps and directions.** If you tap these options, Sello opens the maps app you choose with the photo's coordinates.

None of these services receives your photos, projects or notes, and we receive nothing.

## Sharing and exporting

You decide when to share a photo or export a project (CSV, KML, KMZ, ZIP or PDF). Shared photos carry the stamp and their location metadata; if you prefer, the "Share without EXIF" option removes the metadata without touching the drawn stamp. Whatever you share is subject to the terms of whoever receives it. The app deletes its own copy of each export the next time you open it.

## Children

Sello is a fieldwork tool for adults. It is not directed at children.

## Security

Data stays in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot access (except the photo library copy, if you turn it on). On iOS that folder is also visible in the Files app (On My iPhone › Sello), so you can copy your photos whenever you like. We recommend protecting the phone with a screen lock. If system backups are turned on (Google on Android, iCloud on iOS), the system may include the app's data in them; those backups stay in your Google or Apple account, are governed by their terms, and we have no access to them.

## Retention and deletion

Your data stays on the phone until you delete it. You can delete photos and projects in the app, or delete everything by uninstalling it or clearing its data in the system settings. Copies saved in the photo library are deleted from the photo library. Since we keep no copies, there is nothing else to delete on our side.

## Your rights

Because we do not collect or keep personal data, we hold no information about you to access, correct or delete; you control it directly on the phone. This also covers the rights under Colombia's Law 1581 of 2012 and similar laws elsewhere. For what Google, Apple or OpenStreetMap receive through the optional features, you can exercise your rights with them.

## Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles information, we will update this policy and its effective date before publishing the new version. Previous versions remain in the repository history.
