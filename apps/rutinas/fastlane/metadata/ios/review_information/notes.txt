Rutinas works fully offline and needs no account or sign-in. It makes no network connections.
Notifications are used for habit alerts, reminders and their repeated alerts, "remind me in..." countdowns and, while a workout runs with the screen locked or in another app, the interval changes. The app asks for permission the first time it needs it; without it, everything else keeps working.
The app declares no background modes: while a timer runs in the background its state is computed from the clock, and the remaining interval changes are delivered as scheduled local notifications that are cancelled when the app returns to the foreground.
Voice cues use the device's built-in speech synthesis; all sounds are generated by the app itself.
To try it quickly: on Habits, tap "Habit" and pick a suggestion; on Timer, tap "Workout", choose Tabata and tap "Save and start"; on Reminders, tap "10 min" under "Remind me in...".
