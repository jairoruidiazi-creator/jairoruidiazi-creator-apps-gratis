Rutinas brings your habits, your reminders and an interval timer for workouts together in one app. It is free, with no ads, no subscription and no account, and it works offline.

HABITS
• As many habits as you like, each with its own icon and color.
• Frequencies: every day, specific weekdays, every N days, or N times a week or a month.
• Yes-or-no habits, habits with a goal and a unit (8 glasses of water, 20 pages) or timed habits with a built-in timer (meditate for 10 minutes).
• Habits to quit: count the days without a slip.
• Current and best streak. Days that are not due, days you skip on purpose and an unfinished today never break it.
• A note on each day and a calendar for every habit.
• Alerts at the times you choose, which can repeat until you check the habit off.

REMINDERS
• Once, every N days, on certain weekdays, every month (on a fixed day or the last day) or every year.
• When a month has no 31st, the alert falls on its last day; February 29 falls on the 28th in non-leap years.
• Nagging: the alert repeats every 1 to 60 minutes until you mark it done.
• Quick countdown: "remind me in 10 minutes", and it keeps ringing until you stop it.
• Done and Snooze 10 min right from the notification.

TIMER
• HIIT, Tabata and circuit templates, plus workouts of your own.
• Repeating blocks, nested inside each other (sets of rounds).
• A big screen with each interval's color, what comes next, the current round and the total time left.
• The system voice announces every interval and counts down 3, 2, 1, in English or Spanish.
• Built-in sounds: beep, bell, horn and gong.
• Keeps going with the screen off or in another app: interval changes arrive as notifications with sound.
• A lock against accidental pauses, and the screen stays on while you train.
• Stopwatch with laps (fastest and slowest highlighted) and saved countdowns.

SUMMARY
• Week, month and year: each habit's completion rate, days done, best day, minutes of timed habits and workouts finished.

AND ALSO
• Light, dark or system theme, and eight accent colors.
• Works with TalkBack, large text and generous touch targets.
• Backup to a file, to move your data to another phone.
• In English and Spanish.

PRIVACY
Rutinas has no accounts or servers and does not even request internet access: your habits, reminders and workouts stay on your phone. There are no ads and no analytics. The sounds are generated by the app, with no third-party samples. Rutinas is open source.
